Caring for your plushie

Plush Co. · Care guide · Draft 3

A plushie that is loved gets dirty, and that is fine. This guide covers washing, drying and the small repairs that keep a well-loved friend together for years.

Washing

Most plushies can go in the washing machien on a cold, gentle cycle. Put them in a pillowcase first so buttons and tags survive the spin.

  • Cold water, gentle cycle
  • No bleach, no softener
  • Pillowcase or laundry bag

Repairs

A loose seam is a five-minute fix with a ladder stitch. Eyes and noses are sewn on, never glued, so they can be replaced.
